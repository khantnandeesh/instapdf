How to rotate a PDF

  1. Add your PDFDrop one or more PDF files on the page, or choose them from your device. Nothing is uploaded.
  2. Rotate the pagesTurn all, odd or even pages left or right, or use the arrows on a single page.
  3. SaveSelect Rotate PDF and download the result. Several files come back as one ZIP.

Questions

How do I rotate a PDF and save it permanently?

Rotate the pages here, then select Rotate PDF and download the file. The new orientation is saved in the PDF itself, so it opens the same way in every viewer.

Does rotating a PDF reduce its quality?

No. Only the page orientation setting changes. Text, images and fonts are not re-rendered or recompressed.

Can I rotate just one page?

Yes. Each page has its own rotate buttons, and you can also turn only the odd or the even pages.

Are my files uploaded?

No. Rotation runs in your browser, so your files stay on your device.

Can I rotate a password-protected PDF?

Yes. Enter the password when asked and the file is rotated in your browser.

How many files can I rotate at once?

Up to 20 PDFs at a time, up to 100 MB each and 1,000 pages in total. It is free, with no sign-up and no watermark.
