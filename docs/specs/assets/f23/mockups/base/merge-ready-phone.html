How to merge PDF files

  1. Add your filesDrop PDFs and images on the page, or choose them from your device. Nothing is uploaded.
  2. Put them in orderDrag the files, or switch to Pages to move, rotate, remove or add single pages.
  3. Merge and savePick any options, select Merge PDFs and download one combined PDF.

Questions

How do I combine PDF files into one?

Add the files, put them in the order you want, then select Merge PDFs and download the result. You get one PDF with every page in that order.

Can I change the order of pages, not just files?

Yes. The Pages view shows every page. Drag pages between files, rotate or remove them, or add blank pages and pages from another file.

Can I merge images with PDFs?

Yes. JPG, PNG, GIF, BMP, WebP and TIFF images become pages of the merged PDF, each centred on its own page.

Are my files uploaded?

No. Merging runs in your browser, so your files stay on your device.

Are bookmarks and form fields kept?

Bookmarks are kept by default, and you can also add one per file or remove them. Form fields are kept, with repeated names renamed; you can also share, flatten or remove them.

Can I add a table of contents?

Yes. Choose a contents page with file names or document titles. It links to the first page of each file.

How many files can I merge at once?

Up to 50 files at a time, up to 100 MB each, 500 MB and 1,000 pages in total. It is free, with no sign-up and no watermark.
