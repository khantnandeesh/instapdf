About Us - Sejda.com
Hello, we're Sejda.
Building PDF tools since 2010
Sejda makes it super easy to edit PDF documents.
With 30+ PDF tools, Sejda helps people around the world with their tasks.
Sejda was started in 2010 as an open source project, and we've been improving our PDF tools ever since.
Sejda is based in Amsterdam, the Netherlands.
Sejda is pronounced
/sai duh/
and means 'silk' in the italian dialect of Emilia-Romagna, the land of parmigiano, prosciutto and one of our founders.
Our mission is to empower businesses and individuals to be more productive with PDFs.
We're grateful for our amazing customers, they helped make everything possible.
Let's build a 100-year company together.
